My recommendation

Concept 1, Loose Ends. Runner-up: Concept 3, Pinned Paper.

Loose Ends is the only one where the style itself says the channel name: every line is left open. That rule is simple enough to apply to any character, prop or background, and nobody else is using it. It is also the cheapest to animate, because limbs are single lines.

Pinned Paper is the stronger choice if you want a richer, more crafted look and accept slower production. Stack is the safest for tiny screens but the least distinctive. These are direction sketches; the chosen one gets refined before any rigging starts.
